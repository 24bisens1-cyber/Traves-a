A QUICK NOTE

A few things to know.

TRAVESÍA brings together destination discovery, editable trip plans, budget calculations, map exploration, transport comparisons and animated travel companions. Some results come from live providers; example figures are labelled where live data is unavailable.

Live flight comparison is available through Google Flights and ixigo without a TRAVESÍA key; optional in-app offers need Amadeus credentials. Train search and checkout open official IRCTC or IRCTC-listed ixigo. Example figures are estimates, never live quotes.
